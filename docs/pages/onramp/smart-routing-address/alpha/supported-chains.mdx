## Cross-chain conversions

Cross-chain deposits are delivered as the same token unless the pair is listed here:

- **USDC → USDG on Robinhood** — deposit USDC on Ethereum, Optimism, Arbitrum, Base, Polygon, HyperEVM, World Chain, Unichain, Linea, Ink or Monad, and receive USDG on Robinhood.
- **USDG on Robinhood → USDC** — deposit USDG on Robinhood and receive USDC on any of the chains above.
